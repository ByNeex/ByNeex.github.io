Lycée Notre Dame, Challans

Before 2023

Where It All Started: 9th Grade

Collège St Joseph, Challans

Going all the way back to middle school might seem like a stretch, but that’s really where it all began. Back then, I was a young gamer who loved to tinker: I created custom texture packs for Minecraft and messed around with the Windows command prompt to make "fake viruses". Without even realizing it, gaming was already pushing me toward tech.

The real turning point was a school field trip to the Wild Code School in Nantes. Hearing about the web, data, and VS Code was a revelation. That very night, I downloaded Sublime Text, went on OpenClassrooms, and wrote my first lines of code.

I then did an observation internship at the Challans City Hall IT department. I took PCs apart and put them back together, and got my hands on my first programming languages (HTML, CSS, PHP, JS)... to the point where I coded my entire internship report as a website (it only ran locally, but I was incredibly proud of it!). That year paved the direct path to my Math/CS High School major.
