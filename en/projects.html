Research Internship at IRIT
AI & Medical Imaging

Diffusion ModelsUltrasoundResearch Internship

I am currently completing a research internship at the Institut de Recherche en Informatique de Toulouse (IRIT). My work focuses on using AI to generate synthetic ultrasound images through Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Models (DDPM). The goal is to address the lack of available data in hospitals to better train future diagnostic software. Putting computer science at the service of doctors to assist them daily is what truly drives me in this project ! My experiments and final report are currently in progress... stay tuned for more!

Enedis Hackathon : Data Imputation

Machine LearningTime SeriesMissing Data

Our team participated in this Enedis Hackathon as part of a project for our Artificial Intelligence course. Our mission was to reconstruct missing data across thousands of electricity consumption curves. For this challenge, Enedis provided ultra-realistic consumption profiles (generated by their DeepCourbogen tool) but with intentional "gaps" in the data. The challenge lay in analyzing these time series and developing the best approach to predict and fill these gaps as accurately as possible.
